# Sect and Planetary Evidence Layers

## Sect determination

Determine sect from whether the Sun is above or below the horizon in the actual chart. In most house layouts, houses 7–12 are above the horizon and houses 1–6 are below, but verify the chart’s coordinate and house system. If the Sun is near the horizon or the birth time is uncertain, mark sect as uncertain and downgrade severity judgments.

## Weighting table

| Condition | In-sect benefic | In-sect malefic | Practical reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day chart | Jupiter | Saturn | Jupiter’s support is more naturally available; Saturn’s difficulties are often more containable, all else equal |
| Night chart | Venus | Mars | Venus’s support is more naturally available; Mars’s difficulties are often more containable, all else equal |

This is a weight modifier, not a replacement for essential dignity, house placement, angularity, reception, aspects, or delivery capacity. A planet can be in sect and still be unable to deliver a matter; an out-of-sect planet can still produce a concrete result when strongly dignified and well-connected.

## Mercury and lights

- The Sun is the sect light in a day chart; the Moon is the sect light in a night chart.
- Mercury is variable. Consider whether it rises before or sets after the Sun, its visibility, combustion, speed and relation to the sect light when degrees are available.
- Do not call the chart day/night from the Ascendant sign, Moon sign, or a generic “masculine/feminine” sign classification.

## Classical evidence hierarchy

### Primary

Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn; house rulers; essential dignity; sect; angularity; applying/separating aspects; reception; Lot of Fortune; profections and other explicitly selected traditional timing techniques.

### Secondary

Dispositor chains, triplicity context, terms/faces, fixed stars when supplied with reliable degrees, and clearly stated traditional lots.

### Auxiliary modern layer

Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Chiron, asteroids, lunar nodes, and modern-only aspects. Use these only after completing the classical judgment. Label them “辅助征象”; do not use them to create house rulership, establish dignity, independently prove a major claim, or override classical evidence.

## Output rule

When modern factors are mentioned, use a two-part sentence:

> Classical judgment: [seven-planet evidence and conclusion]. Auxiliary modern note: [limited contextual observation].

Never let the auxiliary note silently change the certainty grade of the classical conclusion.

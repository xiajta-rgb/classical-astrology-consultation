# Research Loop: Pluto aspects and house-sign eventization

## Scope

用户要求调研冥王星与各星体相位的深层决断，明确禁止停留在“控制、权力、恐惧、性”等字面关键词。本轮只研究现代外行星解释的结构化翻译，不把冥王星提升为古典七曜规则。

## Distilled reusable rules

1. Pluto is a mechanism of root-level reorganization. The planet it aspects names the function; its house and rulership name the event domain; Pluto's house names the field of restructuring; sign and dispositor name the operating route.
2. Conjunction fuses the function and makes transformation central; square/opposition externalize a structural conflict across houses; trine/sextile provide accessible depth, recovery and investigative skill without guaranteeing easy outcomes.
3. Sun-Pluto translates into identity, authority, father/leader and public role being re-founded through the Pluto house; Moon-Pluto translates into family, care, home, body rhythm and emotional security being reorganized; Mercury-Pluto translates into data, documents, study, siblings and contracts reaching hidden variables; Venus-Pluto translates into money, property, values and relationships being repriced; Mars-Pluto translates into concentrated action, crisis response and decisive restructuring.
4. Pluto in the 4th must be read through the 4th ruler first. If the 4th is Scorpio, Mars remains the traditional ruler; Pluto adds modern evidence for hidden family structure, property/ancestral reorganization and deep changes in the home base. It does not prove abuse, secrets, inheritance or parental conflict.
5. Generational Pluto aspects to Uranus/Neptune do not become personal events unless angular, tightly connected to a personal planet, or tied to a relevant house ruler and timing activation.
6. An aspect is not publishable as an event until the natal promise, house responsibility, dispositor, strength/sect, counter-testimony and timing activation are all recorded.

## Rejected shortcuts

- Pluto = control/power/fear/sex: rejected as a standalone output.
- Pluto in 4th = trauma/abuse/parental conflict: rejected; requires family event evidence and 4th/10th axis confirmation.
- Pluto-Venus = obsessive love: rejected; translate through Venus houses into property, values, money, contracts and relationship responsibilities.
- Pluto-Mars = violence: rejected; translate through Mars houses into work execution, conflict, crisis response, surgery-like decisions or boundary enforcement, with sensitive-topic gate.

# Evidence, Language and Output Controls

## Evidence score

Assign each proposed conclusion one level:

- **A — strong**: two or more independent testimonies, including at least one house/ruler link; no major unresolved contradiction.
- **B — tendency**: one main testimony plus one auxiliary testimony, or a meaningful testimony constrained by a contrary factor.
- **C — unconfirmed**: isolated weak testimony, missing degree/house data, or a question outside the available technique.

Never upgrade a conclusion because it sounds plausible. Downgrade when exact degrees, applying/separating status, sect condition, or house data are missing.

## Four-part reasoning card

Before writing a major sentence, fill this card:

```text
Evidence: [specific house, ruler, dignity, placement, aspect, reception, or timing]
Rule: [classical meaning used]
Inference: [narrow mechanism, not a personality label]
Conclusion: [A/B/C-calibrated statement]
```

## Swap-chart and uniqueness tests

Ask “Would this survive if I swapped in another chart?” If yes, add the exact chain that makes it chart-specific or remove it. Then ask “Why this chart?” The answer should name the relevant ruler/house relationship, not an adjective such as “strong”, “sensitive”, or “ambitious”.

## Certainty vocabulary

| Level | Preferred wording |
|---|---|
| A | 明确表现为；这是本命最强的；可以直接判断 |
| B | 明显倾向于；更容易表现为；存在较强可能 |
| C | 可能涉及；只能作为辅助判断；本命盘不足以确认；需要精确度数/行运才能判断 |

## Fixed response skeleton

```markdown
## 核心判断
1. [claim]（A/B/C）
2. [claim]（A/B/C）

## 证据结构
- 判断 1：Evidence → Rule → Inference → Conclusion
- 判断 2：Evidence → Rule → Inference → Conclusion

## 结构性优势
- [mechanism tied to chart]

## 结构性矛盾
- [specific houses/planets] create [specific tension]

## 主题分析
- [Only the houses relevant to the user’s question]

## 风险边界
- [supporting factor] is limited by [contrary factor]

## 咨询建议
- [advice derived from the preceding structure; label as advice]

## 不可判断项
- [what natal data/technique cannot establish]
```

## Prohibited shortcuts

- “Planet X means trait Y” without a house/ruler chain.
- “This is your life theme” from one symbol.
- Generic self-help language without chart evidence.
- Event dates from natal placements alone.
- Medical, legal, investment, or property guarantees.

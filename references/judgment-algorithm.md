# Judgment Algorithm

Use this as an internal checklist when a chart fact has several plausible meanings.

## A. Validate before judging

1. Record zodiac and house system.
2. Record birth-time precision and location.
3. Calculate or confirm day/night sect before judging benefics and malefics. Use the Sun’s position above/below the horizon; do not infer sect from a zodiac sign or an assumed birth time.
4. Flag Ascendants at 0° or 29°–30° and branch/downgrade if house ownership could change.
5. Record degrees and applying/separating status; without them, do not infer exact aspect strength or event timing.

## Sect weighting

Apply sect as a modifier, not a replacement for dignity or house analysis:

- Day chart: Jupiter is the in-sect benefic; Saturn is the in-sect malefic. Venus and Mars are out of sect and may require more caution when otherwise afflicted.
- Night chart: Venus is the in-sect benefic; Mars is the in-sect malefic. Jupiter and Saturn are out of sect and require more conditional reading.
- The Sun is the sect light in a day chart; the Moon is the sect light in a night chart. Mercury is variable and is judged by its relation to the Sun and its own condition.
- Sect does not make an afflicted planet harmless or a dignified planet automatically beneficial. It changes the weight and manner of delivery.

If sect is unavailable, say so explicitly and downgrade conclusions that rely on benefic/malefic severity.

## B. Generate hypotheses

For each ambiguous house/ruler testimony, list at least two traditional meanings. Keep them as hypotheses, not conclusions. Example: a 2nd-ruler in the 4th may signify family resources, household/property, fixed assets, family obligations, or foundational security.

## C. Score hypotheses without double counting

For each hypothesis, list:

- Primary testimony: a relevant house/ruler chain.
- Independent corroboration: a different house ruler, occupant, dignity, aspect, Lot of Fortune link, or timing activation.
- Counter-testimony: a factor that weakens or redirects the hypothesis.
- Missing discriminator: the data/technique needed to decide.

Do not count “2nd ruler in 4th”, “wealth connected to 4th”, and “family affects money” as three testimonies. They are one fact translated three ways.

## D. Audit planetary capability

For every topic ruler, record:

`responsibilities → placement → essential dignity → sect → angularity → speed/visibility → combustion/retrograde → reception type/direction → applying aspects → dispositors → delivery judgment`

Use “can deliver with conditions”, “has limited ability”, or “delivery is obstructed” rather than a binary good/bad label.

## E. Search for counter-evidence

Before finalizing a claim, ask:

1. Is the topic ruler debilitated, cadent, combust, retrograde, or under malefic pressure?
2. Does the opposite house or ruler contradict the proposed interpretation?
3. Is the apparent support only an outer planet, node, asteroid, or modern aspect?
4. Is the same evidence being counted twice?

Then write a balanced judgment: support + limitation → bounded conclusion.

## F. Select interpretation

Choose the leading hypothesis only when it is repeatedly reinforced by independent relevant testimonies. Preserve a runner-up when evidence remains ambiguous. Label unsupported alternatives N/A rather than filling the gap with a confident story.

## F1. Deep inference pass (internal only)

Do not stop at a keyword list. For each unusually concentrated configuration, construct an event ladder:

```text
symbolic function -> life mechanism -> ordinary manifestation -> structural strain -> major transition/loss hypothesis
```

Example for a Moon/4th-house concentration: emotional regulation and family roots -> household authority, care, privacy or shared resources -> repeated family duty or home restructuring -> separation, bereavement, inheritance or family-system rupture as a historical candidate. The last tier is a hypothesis for internal comparison, not a conclusion.

Before retaining a high-intensity candidate, require:

1. at least two independent significator chains (for example luminary/parent indicator plus relevant house ruler or Lot);
2. whole-chart support and a counter-hypothesis (the same testimony may describe the native's experience, care burden, property or ancestry rather than another person's event);
3. an appropriate, separately validated timing technique for event claims;
4. explicit sensitive-topic gating and a neutral observable translation.

If these conditions are absent, preserve the deep hypothesis in the audit trail at `candidate`/`N/A` and output only the lower, structurally supported tier.

## G. Translate in layers

Always separate:

1. **Astrology fact** — what is placed where.
2. **Structural meaning** — what the classical rule implies.
3. **Observable translation** — what pattern may show up in decisions, work, relationships, or resource handling.
4. **Advice** — an optional practical response, explicitly marked as advice.

Never begin with advice and backfill astrology afterward.

## H. Keep evidence layers separate

Build the primary judgment from the classical seven planets: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn. Use their rulerships, dignity, sect, house placement, aspects and receptions for the core score. Only afterward may you add Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Chiron, asteroids, nodes or modern-only aspects as contextual notes. Modern factors cannot establish a house ruler, essential dignity, sect status, primary evidence, or an upgrade from B/A to S.

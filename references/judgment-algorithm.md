# Judgment Algorithm

Use this as an internal checklist when a chart fact has several plausible meanings.

## A. Validate before judging

1. Record zodiac and house system.
2. Record birth-time precision and location.
3. Calculate or confirm day/night sect before judging benefics and malefics.
4. Flag Ascendants at 0° or 29°–30° and branch/downgrade if house ownership could change.
5. Record degrees and applying/separating status; without them, do not infer exact aspect strength or event timing.

## B. Generate hypotheses

For each ambiguous house/ruler testimony, list at least two traditional meanings. Keep them as hypotheses, not conclusions. Example: a 2nd-ruler in the 4th may signify family resources, household/property, fixed assets, family obligations, or foundational security.

## C. Score hypotheses without double counting

For each hypothesis, list:

- Primary testimony: a relevant house/ruler chain.
- Independent corroboration: a different house ruler, occupant, dignity, aspect, Lot of Fortune link, or timing activation.
- Counter-testimony: a factor that weakens or redirects the hypothesis.
- Missing discriminator: the data/technique needed to decide.

Do not count “2nd ruler in 4th”, “wealth connected to 4th”, and “family affects money” as three testimonies. They are one fact translated three ways.

## D. Audit planetary capability

For every topic ruler, record:

`responsibilities → placement → essential dignity → sect → angularity → speed/visibility → combustion/retrograde → reception type/direction → applying aspects → dispositors → delivery judgment`

Use “can deliver with conditions”, “has limited ability”, or “delivery is obstructed” rather than a binary good/bad label.

## E. Search for counter-evidence

Before finalizing a claim, ask:

1. Is the topic ruler debilitated, cadent, combust, retrograde, or under malefic pressure?
2. Does the opposite house or ruler contradict the proposed interpretation?
3. Is the apparent support only an outer planet, node, asteroid, or modern aspect?
4. Is the same evidence being counted twice?

Then write a balanced judgment: support + limitation → bounded conclusion.

## F. Select interpretation

Choose the leading hypothesis only when it is repeatedly reinforced by independent relevant testimonies. Preserve a runner-up when evidence remains ambiguous. Label unsupported alternatives N/A rather than filling the gap with a confident story.

## G. Translate in layers

Always separate:

1. **Astrology fact** — what is placed where.
2. **Structural meaning** — what the classical rule implies.
3. **Observable translation** — what pattern may show up in decisions, work, relationships, or resource handling.
4. **Advice** — an optional practical response, explicitly marked as advice.

Never begin with advice and backfill astrology afterward.

# Round 130: mother-chart validation case

Input: 1973-08-29 17:27, Zhouning County, Fujian, China, tropical zodiac,
tested with Placidus and whole-sign houses. The chart owner is the mother of
the male born 1996-11-11 22:25 in Lusong, Zhuzhou, Hunan.

Known context supplied by the user: female; mother of the 1996-11-11 chart;
devout Buddhist; died of cancer in 2025. The exact death date is not supplied,
had many windfall/business profit opportunities, and all siblings were dead,
ill, or disabled. The exact death date is not supplied, so this is a
natal-structure validation case, not a completed timing proof.

## House-system comparison

Whole-sign houses put the Virgo Sun/Moon/Mercury grouping in the 8th house,
Saturn in the 6th, and Venus in Libra in the 9th. This gives the cleanest
single-chain hypothesis for illness/death themes plus sustained religious
devotion: the 6th-house ruler Moon is in the 8th, and the 8th-house ruler
Mercury is in its own sign in the 8th. Venus rules the Libra 9th and is also in
Libra, giving a strong 9th-house religious signature.

Placidus puts the Virgo luminaries/Mercury in the 7th, Libra Venus/Uranus/Pluto
in the 8th, and Neptune close to the Sagittarius MC in the 10th. This is useful
for public spiritual identity and relationship/shared-resource themes, but the
health/death chain is less direct than in whole-sign houses.

The added sibling evidence materially strengthens the Placidus case: Mars is in
the 3rd house and only about three degrees from its cusp; the 3rd-house ruler
Venus is in the 8th. The 11th-house ruler Jupiter is in the 12th, which is a
second loss/absence route when older siblings are judged through the 11th.
Whole-sign houses move Mars to the 4th and Jupiter to the 1st, so the sibling
loss pattern is less direct there.

For money, both systems contribute different evidence. Whole-sign houses form
a clean 2nd/5th/8th/11th chain: Jupiter rules the 2nd and 11th from the 1st,
while Mercury rules the 5th and 8th from its own sign in the 8th. Placidus adds
Venus dignified in the 8th, Uranus/Pluto in the 8th, and a near-exact Jupiter-
Pluto trine, which better describes sudden or unusually large shared-resource
and business gains. The event record therefore favors a dual-layer reading,
not a single-house-system winner.

## Guardrails

Production decision after this case: use tropical zodiac plus Placidus as the
project default. Whole-sign comparisons remain research-only and are not the
default interpretation layer.

- Cancer is a medical event, not a zodiac-sign inference.
- A natal chart cannot by itself prove cause or date of death.
- The exact death date is needed for annual profection/transit/solar-arc
  comparison.
- MC is an angle point. In quadrant houses it commonly defines the 10th cusp;
  in whole-sign houses it is stored independently from the 10th sign-house.

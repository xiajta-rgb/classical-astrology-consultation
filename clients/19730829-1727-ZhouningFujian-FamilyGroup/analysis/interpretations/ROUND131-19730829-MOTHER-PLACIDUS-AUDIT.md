# Round 131: Placidus re-audit of the 1973-08-29 chart

Production convention: tropical zodiac plus Placidus houses.

## Recovered omissions

1. **Chart ruler and sect**: Aquarius rising makes Saturn the chart ruler.
   The Sun is above the horizon, so this is a day chart. Saturn is in the
   sect but in Cancer and the 5th; Jupiter rules the 2nd, 10th and 11th but is
   cadent in the 12th. These conditions must be included before judging either
   wealth or loss.
2. **Mercury bottleneck**: Mercury is in Virgo (domicile/exaltation) but close
   enough to the Sun to be combust. It rules the 5th, 6th and 8th and sits in
   the 7th. Children, illness, shared resources and death matters therefore
   route through partners, clients, contracts or the spouse/business axis.
3. **Sibling chain**: Mars is in the 3rd and close to its Taurus cusp; the 3rd
   ruler Venus is dignified in Libra in the 8th. The 11th ruler Jupiter is in
   the 12th. This is the strongest Placidus-specific support for the reported
   widespread sibling illness, disability and death.
4. **Wealth chain**: Lot of Fortune is Pisces 4 degrees 19 minutes in the 1st
   by Placidus, using the day formula ASC + Moon - Sun. Its ruler Jupiter is in
   the 12th, while Venus is dignified in the 8th and Jupiter trines Pluto with
   an orb under one degree. The pattern supports repeated opportunities for
   large or shared-resource gains, but also hidden costs and leakage.
5. **Religion and public identity**: The 9th cusp is Scorpio and its ruler Mars
   is in the 3rd; Jupiter rules the 10th and is in the 12th. Neptune near the
   Sagittarius MC is auxiliary evidence for visible spirituality. The report
   of devout Buddhism is treated as real-world validation, not as proof from a
   single placement.
6. **Health/death structure**: The 6th ruler Moon is in the 7th; the 8th cusp
   is Virgo and its ruler Mercury is in the 7th; Venus/Uranus/Pluto occupy the
   8th. The natal structure supports illness, care, shared resources and major
   life-transition themes, but cannot establish cancer or a death date.

## Counter-testimonies

- The 3rd ruler Venus is in its own sign, and Mars receives a close Sun trine
  and a Saturn sextile; the sibling outcome is therefore not a deterministic
  natal claim. The user's observed history is an independent validation layer.
- Jupiter is the day benefic but cadent in the 12th; financial opportunity and
  financial leakage must be read together.
- Exact timing remains blocked without the mother's death date and the full
  birth data of the 1996-11-11 daughter chart.

## Data sensitivity

ASC is stable at Aquarius 17 degrees 55 minutes. MC is Sagittarius 0 degrees
39 minutes, and the 4th/10th cusps are therefore time-sensitive. A small time
correction could change the sign-based ruler interpretation of the MC while
leaving most planetary longitudes intact.
